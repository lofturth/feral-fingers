# Project instructions

Follow the workspace's general Codex instructions and `how-I-work/` guidance.

- Keep all Feral Fingers work open source. Do not add private material or secrets.
- Favor rapid, focused experiments and learn from real use.
- Keep implementations simple. Avoid unnecessary abstractions, infrastructure, and premature production hardening.
- Preserve useful experiments alongside longer-term development; promote ideas only when they prove valuable.
